Magpie is a podcast and article player that can be operated entirely by voice, in the app or through Siri. It is designed so that blind and partially sighted users can use every feature without sight, and it can also be used with visual controls. It will not review like an ordinary media app: the library starts empty and is populated through voice commands. Written articles are read aloud with Apple system voices through the standard audio player, and every article links to the original web page.

Please sign in with your own Apple ID or Google account. Both methods create a Magpie account automatically; there is no separate Magpie username or password or demo account. Apple and Google are both available on the first screen. Settings → Sign-in Methods connects the other provider after authentication and combines any existing Magpie libraries, so either method can access the same library.

Suggested path, which takes about two minutes:

1. Sign in with Apple on the first screen.
2. Tap the microphone button at the top right. Read and allow the AI data-sharing screen, then allow the microphone and speech-recognition prompts.
3. Say “subscribe to In Our Time”. The app searches, subscribes and confirms aloud. The show now appears in the Following tab.
4. Tap the microphone again and say “play the latest In Our Time”. The app confirms what it found and starts playing.
5. Tap the microphone and say “pause”, then “carry on”.

Following has a search field for existing feeds and an Add Sources (+) button for finding public podcasts or adding feed, homepage and Apple Podcasts sharing links. If a publication is absent from the public directory, the explicit Search the web button offers the consented AI fallback. Episodes can also be played by tapping them.

An account is required because the listener’s library and listening positions are stored server-side to follow them between devices. Account deletion is available in Settings and also revokes a connected Sign in with Apple authorization.

To test saved articles, open a public article in Safari, choose Share → Magpie, review the title and text preview, and save it. Open Saved in Magpie to read or listen. You can also add a link directly in Saved. Sharing an article again updates its saved copy. Supported YouTube and Vimeo embeds play only when tapped and offer an Open video in browser fallback.

Newsletters that only exist as email are supported through a private inbound address. Settings → Newsletters shows the address for the signed-in account, created on first view. Any email sent to that address is delivered to the app: the first message from a new sender appears under “Waiting for your answer” at the top of the Latest tab, with Follow and Block buttons, and nothing from it is read until it has been followed. To test, send any email from your own account to the address shown in Settings and reopen Latest shortly afterwards. Following the sender turns it into a show in Following and reads its messages aloud like any article. When a pasted web address in Add Sources has no feed, a Sign up by email button asks the newsletter's own sign-up form to send its issues to that address instead. Mail is received by a Cloudflare Email Routing worker and handed to our server, which keeps the message so it can be read aloud; blocking a sender discards its mail unread.

Speech is converted to text on the device. Only the text is sent to our server, never an audio recording. Before the first AI request, the app explicitly asks permission to send that text and recent episode details to OpenAI. Permission can be withdrawn under Settings → Privacy & Support. The privacy policy describes this in full.
