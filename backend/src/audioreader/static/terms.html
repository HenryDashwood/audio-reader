Magpie subscription terms

Updated 27 October 2026.

Magpie Plus

Magpie Plus includes up to 500 PDF pages per billing month. The current price and renewal period appear in your app store’s purchase confirmation. Unused pages do not roll over. Each PDF may contain up to 200 pages and be up to 25 MiB. Password-protected PDFs are not supported. Storage limits are shown when reached.

Pages are reserved when an import is accepted and count against that billing month. Downloading or rereading an imported PDF does not use more pages. Deleting a PDF does not refund its page allowance. A provider-confirmed failed conversion releases its reservation; an unconfirmed submission may retain the reservation while its outcome is uncertain.

Renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled before renewal through the App Store or Google Play. Manage your subscription in the store where you purchased it. Cancellation stops future renewals; access continues until the paid period ends, unless the store revokes or refunds the purchase. Refund requests are handled by the store under its applicable policies.

Previously imported PDFs remain readable after cancellation while your Magpie account exists. Deleting your Magpie account removes its content and does not cancel your store subscription. Cancel the subscription separately to stop future renewals.

Your documents

Only import material you are entitled to use. Datalab processes PDFs to extract readable content. Conversion can contain mistakes, especially in equations, tables and scans. Check the original document when accuracy matters.
